ddfast design

Illustrated explainers of the techniques behind ddfast, a performance optimization of the DDS double-dummy search, for programmers who know C++ and bridge basics.

One word per hand

Each hand as a single 64-bit word, 16 bits per suit, and the card logic (following suit, trick winners, one card per run of touching cards, the cards that decided a result) as a few bit operations. Worth 1.3-1.4× over DDS on its own.

Follow-hand rules

How the second, third and fourth hands pick their first card with a few cheap rules instead of DDS's per-card weights, with worked examples. The largest single addition: 1.66×.

Endgame tables

Every 2- and 3-card ending as one lookup, and 4-card endings matched against learned card patterns, with examples from the real tables. Worth 1.30× as a pair.
Source and documentation: github.com/randalburns/ddfast
